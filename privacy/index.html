Genita Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026 · Updated November 3, 2026

Genita is an AI video companion for iPhone and Mac, operated by Bagel Labs Corp. ("we", "us"). You create a companion from one photo and talk with it face to face. Genita is currently a free, non-commercial preview. This policy explains what data Genita uses, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier and the email address Apple shares with us. If you choose "Hide My Email", this is an Apple relay address. We may also receive the name you allow Apple to share, and we store the name you enter in the app.
  • The photo you upload. We use it to create your companion's look, voice choices and animated face. We also store the companion media generated from it (images, facial expressions and mouth shapes used to animate the face).
  • Camera and voice during calls. While a call is on, your voice is streamed to transcribe what you say, and when you ask about something you show, a few still frames from your camera are sent to answer the question. This audio and these frames are processed only to answer you and are not kept as recordings.
  • Your style and memories. If you paste a reply from an AI assistant you already use, we keep only the style summary made from it, not the pasted text. Genita can also remember things from your conversations (for example, short notes about what you talked about). These are stored with your account until you delete them or your account.
  • Basic technical data. Device type, app version, your device’s time zone (so your companion knows your local time in calls; it is sent to Meta with your messages), and error and usage events needed to run and fix the service.

How we use it

Only to provide Genita: to sign you in, create and animate your companion, hold the conversation, answer questions about what your camera shows, move a call between your iPhone and Mac, remember what you asked it to remember, and keep the service working and safe. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.

Who processes it for us

To run Genita we send the minimum data needed to these service providers:

  • Meta (Meta Model API): AI models for conversation, image understanding, speech recognition and image generation. Your voice, the photos you upload, camera frames you choose to show and your messages are sent to Meta to produce answers.
  • Amazon Web Services: our servers and storage in the United States (us-east-1), including the GPU servers where we run Meta's open models (for example, the voice model) ourselves. If we use Modal to host those GPU servers, Modal processes the same data for us in the same way.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple.
  • Resend: sign-in emails. Resend receives only your email address and the one-time code.

Use of your inputs by Meta. During the preview, Genita uses Meta Model API credits provided for the Meta AI Developer Hackathon. Under Meta's terms for these credits, content sent to the Meta Model API may be used by Meta, including to improve its models. Do not share anything with Genita that you do not want sent to Meta.

Genita does not send your data to ChatGPT, Claude or any other AI service. We do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.

Where it is stored and for how long

Your account, companion media and memories are stored on Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1) for as long as you keep your account. Call audio and camera frames are not stored after they are processed. Limited server logs are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, your uploaded photo, your companion and its generated media, and your memories from our systems. Data already sent to Meta is handled under Meta's terms and retention rules. You can also email us to ask for deletion or a copy of your data.

Photos of other people

Only upload photos you have the right to use. If you upload a photo of another person, you are responsible for having their permission. Genita refuses photos of well-known public figures and photos you tell us you do not have the right to use.

Not an emergency service

Genita is an AI companion. It does not provide medical, mental health or crisis services and does not monitor conversations for emergencies. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is limited to the people and systems that need it to run Genita. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
