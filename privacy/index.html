Genita Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Genita is an AI video companion for iPhone and Mac, operated by Bagel Labs Corp. ("we", "us"). You create a companion from one photo and talk with it face to face. This policy explains what data Genita uses, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier and the email address Apple shares with us. If you choose "Hide My Email", this is an Apple relay address. We may also receive the name you allow Apple to share, and we store the name you enter in the app.
  • The photo you upload. We use it to create your companion's look, voice choices and animated face. We also store the companion media generated from it (images, short videos and face data used for lip sync).
  • Camera and voice during calls. While a call is on, your voice is streamed to transcribe what you say, and when you ask about something you show, a few still frames from your camera are sent to answer the question. This audio and these frames are processed only to answer you and are not kept as recordings.
  • Memories you keep. Genita can remember things from your conversations (for example, short notes about what you talked about). If you ask it to remember something it saw, it keeps a small cropped image and one line of text, not the original frame. Memories are stored with your account until you delete them or your account.
  • Basic technical data. Device type, app version, and error and usage events needed to run and fix the service.

How we use it

Only to provide Genita: to sign you in, create and animate your companion, hold the conversation, answer questions about what your camera shows, move a call between your iPhone and Mac, remember what you asked it to remember, and keep the service working and safe. We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your personal data for advertising.

Who processes it for us

To run Genita we send the minimum data needed to these service providers, who process it on our behalf:

  • Meta: AI models for conversation, image understanding, speech recognition and image generation.
  • Other AI service providers: voice synthesis and video generation used to give your companion a voice and a moving face.
  • Cloud GPU providers: real-time lip-sync rendering during calls.
  • Amazon Web Services: hosting and storage, in the United States (us-east-1).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple.

The specific providers may change as we improve the service; any provider we use processes your data only to deliver Genita.

Where it is stored and for how long

Your account, companion media and memories are stored on Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1) for as long as you keep your account. Call audio and camera frames are not stored after they are processed. Limited server logs are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, your uploaded photo, your companion and its generated media, and your memories from our systems. Copies held by service providers for processing are deleted under their own retention rules. You can also email us to ask for deletion or a copy of your data.

Photos of other people

Only upload photos you have the right to use. If you upload a photo of another person, you are responsible for having their permission.

Not an emergency service

Genita is an AI companion. It does not provide medical, mental health or crisis services and does not monitor conversations for emergencies. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and access is limited to the people and systems that need it to run Genita. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
